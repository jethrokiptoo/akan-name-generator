THE STORY BEHIND THE NAME

A name is more than a word.

Akan names are deeply connected to Ghanaian culture and tradition. Traditionally, children may receive a name associated with the day of the week on which they were born.

These names are part of a rich cultural tradition that connects identity, family, history, and community.

Sun Kwasi / Akosua
Mon Kwadwo / Adwoa
Tue Kwabena / Abenaa
Wed Kwaku / Akua
Thu Yaw / Yaa
Fri Kofi / Afua
Sat Kwame / Ama
